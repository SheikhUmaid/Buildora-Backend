How to install

The apps aren't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks for permission the first time.

  1. DownloadTap the download button for your app. Your browser saves the .apk file.
  2. Open the fileTap the finished download in the notification bar or the Downloads app.
  3. Allow the installIf Android asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Install & sign inTap Install, open the app, and log in or create your account.

Questions

Which app do I need?

If you're building or renovating a home and want to follow the work, get Buildora Insight. If you're the engineer, contractor or a site manager posting updates, get Buildora On-site.

How do I join my project as a home owner?

Sign up in Buildora Insight, tap Join project and enter the 6-character invite code your contractor shares with you.

I'm a site manager. How do I log in?

Install Buildora On-site, tap Login as Manager and enter the phone number and 4-digit PIN your engineer gave you.

Android says the file might be harmful. Is it safe?

Android shows this warning for every app installed from outside the Play Store. If you downloaded it from this page, tap Install anyway. Play Protect may also offer to scan it, which is fine.

How do I update to a new version?

Download the app again from this page and install it over the old one. Your account and projects stay as they are.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Both apps are Android-only for now.
